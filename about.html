About Us

History: Badr Medical Center was established to serve the community of Farwaniya, Kuwait, with quality and compassionate medical care. Over the years, we’ve grown into a trusted multidisciplinary healthcare provider.

Mission: To continually improve the health of the communities by discovering and delivering compassionate and quality healthcare services through a team of dedicated and highly qualified professionals.

Vision: To be the largest healthcare service provider in the region by catering to the needy at the most affordable rates.

Why Badr? We are known for our exceptional affordability, diverse medical specialties, and compassionate care. Our unique approach focuses on human-centered service without compromising quality.
